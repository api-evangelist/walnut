# Walnut

> Walnut is the AI-powered interactive demo platform for B2B sales and marketing teams. Customers report sales cycles 34% faster, conversions 32% higher, and engagement 26% higher when buyers experience the product through a Walnut demo instead of a slide deck or scheduled live call.

Walnut combines codeless demo creation, AI-driven personalization, and demo analytics into a single platform used across sales, presales, marketing, partnerships, and customer success. Demos are captured directly from a live product (HTML or video), edited without engineering, personalized at scale by AI, and shared as standalone links, embeds, or interactive deal rooms. Buyers can self-serve a guided product experience instead of waiting for a 30-minute live call.

### The 5 AI products
1. **AI Mode** — single-prompt generation of personalized demos.
2. **StoryCaptureAI** — automated capture of a live product into an editable interactive demo (HTML and video).
3. **GuidesCreationAI** — AI-generated step-by-step guides and tooltips inside a demo.
4. **TranslationAI** — one-click localization of a demo into 25+ languages.
5. **InsightsAI** — engagement analytics and AI-summarized buying signals.
