# Walnut Knowledge Base

> Walnut is interactive product demo software for sales teams. This knowledge base covers how to capture, edit, personalize, share, and analyze demos, plus integrations with CRMs and supporting tools.

Every article below links to its raw Markdown source. The full machine-readable catalog with metadata is at https://help.walnut.io/api/v1/docs.json.
